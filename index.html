How to Use the Freelance Fee Calculator

Whether you work on Upwork, Fiverr, or invoice clients directly through PayPal and Stripe, platform fees can take a significant cut from your earnings. Use this tool to plan your pricing accurately.

  • Calculate Net Payout: Enter the total project budget to see how much money will actually hit your bank account after commissions.

Popular Freelance & Payment Fee Rates

Upwork: Charges a flat 10% service fee on all contracts (hourly, fixed-price, and Project Catalog).

Fiverr: Deducts a 20% commission from every completed order and tip.

PayPal & Stripe: Standard online payment processing typically ranges from 2.9% to 4.49% plus a small fixed transaction fee ($0.30–$0.49).
